STOP CONDITIONS
- Available, held, or on-hand cooked inventory becomes negative.
- A removal succeeds above available inventory.
- Waste can be saved without a valid reason.
- A hold release or discard loses the original hold record.
- A blocking hold does not block close.
- A P0/P1 exception closes unresolved.
- An unresolved exception has no owner but close succeeds.
- A blocking count variance closes without manager resolution.
- A correction overwrites original evidence.
- A duplicate command creates another ledger entry or event.
- Cross-tenant data is read, inferred, or changed.
- Validation-only workbench results are represented as durable persistence.
