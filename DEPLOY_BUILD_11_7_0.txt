STOP CONDITIONS
- A report total does not match source transactions.
- An unexplained difference is hidden or marked complete.
- A missing physical count or source ID is ignored.
- Weekly accuracy is calculated as a simple average instead of WAPE.
- Yield uses an unstated or incorrect denominator.
- Waste or ending-inventory rate uses an undocumented denominator.
- Smoker utilization exceeds 100% without blocking.
- An incomplete or unreconciled date influences learning.
- A recommendation exceeds the approved bounds.
- A recommendation auto-applies.
- A Viewer or unauthorized role approves a recommendation.
- Cross-tenant or cross-location data is combined.
- Export omits formulas or lineage.
- Validation-lab results are represented as durable production persistence.
