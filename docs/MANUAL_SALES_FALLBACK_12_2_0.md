# Manual Sales Fallback — Build 12.2.0

Manual fallback remains available during provider outage. It requires tenant, location, business date, reason, source-document reference, actor, lines, and totals.

Entries above $500 require manager approval under the controlled contract. Manual records are reportable but excluded from automatic forecast learning.

A manual day cannot silently overwrite or replace a provider-imported day. The collision requires explicit reconciliation.
