STOP CONDITIONS
- P0 is deferred by quiet hours.
- An inactive or cross-tenant recipient receives a notification.
- A disabled recipient channel receives a notification.
- Duplicate routing creates another delivery.
- Retry exceeds four attempts or loses the failed delivery.
- Dead-letter evidence is absent.
- Acknowledgement does not stop future escalation.
- Provider health exposes a credential or secret value.
- Viewer or unauthorized role changes administration settings.
- Admin audit overwrites before/after evidence.
- Support bundle contains a password, PIN, session token, access token, application secret, or customer personal data.
- Validation-lab results are represented as live provider delivery or durable persistence.
